# Delete Your Rafiq Account

Rafiq allows users to permanently delete their account and associated data directly from the application.

## How to Delete Your Account

To delete your Rafiq account:

1. Open the Rafiq application.
2. Go to **Settings**.
3. Select **Delete Account**.
4. Confirm the account deletion.

Once the deletion is confirmed, your account and the data associated with it will be permanently deleted.

## Data That Will Be Deleted

Deleting your account will permanently remove the data associated with your account, including:

- Your personal information and profile data.
- Information about the doctors associated with your account.
- Your learning and assessment data.
- Your personalized learning plan.
- Your progress and activity data.
- Other data associated with your account.

## Data Retention

Rafiq does not retain the above personal data after the account deletion is completed.

Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.
