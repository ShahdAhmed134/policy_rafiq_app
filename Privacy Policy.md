# Privacy Policy

## Introduction

Rafiq – Autism Support for Kids is committed to protecting the privacy and security of our users and their children. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our application.

## Information We Collect

Depending on your role, we may collect:

### Parents and Children

- Name, email address, and phone number
- Child's name and age
- Assessment and relevant health or developmental information
- Learning progress and activity data
- Messages with the assigned doctor

### Doctors

- Name, email address, and phone number
- Professional credentials and license information
- Clinic information and availability
- Treatment plans and session notes
- Messages with patients' families

## How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

- Create and manage accounts
- Provide personalized learning and support
- Conduct assessments and track progress
- Manage treatment and learning plans
- Enable communication between parents and assigned doctors
- Improve and secure our services

We do not sell personal information or use children's information for advertising.

## Child Data Protection

We take children's privacy seriously. Child-related information is accessible only to authorized users, such as the child's parent or guardian and assigned doctor.

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children without appropriate parental or guardian involvement.

## Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or loss. These measures include secure data storage, encryption during transmission, and access controls.

However, no electronic system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal information. Information may only be shared when necessary to provide our services, comply with legal requirements, maintain security, or with appropriate authorization.

## Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of their account and applicable personal or child-related information by contacting us.

## Your Rights

You may request to access, correct, or delete your personal information, subject to applicable legal requirements.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in this policy.
